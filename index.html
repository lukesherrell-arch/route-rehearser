        How it works

        1. Type where you start and where you're going, then Find route.
        2. If it's not the way your instructor goes, tap + Via point then tap the road it should use. Drag a numbered pin to move it.
        3. Rehearse walks you along the route in Street View. Step with ⏮ ⏭ (or arrow keys), or press ▶ to play.
        4. You can drag the picture to look around. Recentre faces you back down the road.
        5. Grey on the slider = no Street View there, so it skips that bit. Orange marks = checkpoints.
        6. The banner at the top shows the next manoeuvre and which lane. The small map follows you; tap it to make it bigger.
        7. Checkpoints are added automatically at each junction with a tip. Use 📍 Pin here to add your own (box junctions, tricky bits).
        8. Routes are saved on this device only. My routes → Export makes a backup file you can import on your other device.
